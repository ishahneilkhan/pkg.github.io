FAQ

Business package, clarified.

Is ৳999 a monthly subscription?

Yes. Business is a recurring monthly subscription priced at ৳999/month. Included managed services remain active while the subscription is active.

Does Business include an admin panel?

Yes. Business is designed to include a dedicated admin panel for supported website management features.

Can I accept payments?

Payment workflows can be integrated depending on the website and selected payment provider. Manual payment methods such as bKash, Nagad and bank transfer can also be supported where appropriate.

Is payment gateway setup always included?

Gateway integration depends on the selected provider, merchant requirements and technical availability. Provider-specific fees or merchant charges may apply.

Can I connect WhatsApp and Messenger?

Yes. Business supports communication integrations such as WhatsApp and Messenger where the selected website supports them.

Is advanced SEO guaranteed to rank my website?

No package can guarantee a specific search ranking. Advanced SEO means the website receives a stronger technical and on-page SEO foundation.

Can I upgrade to Premium later?

Yes. If your business eventually needs a more advanced e-commerce or business management system, you can move to the Premium package.
