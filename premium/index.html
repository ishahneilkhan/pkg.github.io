FAQ

Questions before going Premium?

Is hosting included?

Yes. Premium includes managed hosting and SSL while your monthly subscription remains active.

Is inventory management really included for free?

Yes. Inventory management is included in the Premium package. Stock in/out, adjustments, low-stock alerts and out-of-stock handling are part of the package.

Can I sell products online?

Yes. Premium is designed for e-commerce and business websites with products, cart, checkout, customer management and orders.

Can I connect my own domain?

Yes. You can connect your custom domain to your Premium website.

Can payment gateways be added?

Yes. Payment integration can be added based on the available gateway, provider requirements and your business needs. Manual payment methods can also be configured.

Can the website be customized later?

Yes. Premium supports ongoing content updates, maintenance, minor customization and custom feature development according to the agreed scope.

Is the monthly payment an installment?

No. This is a monthly subscription for the managed website service. The website hosting and included services remain active while the subscription is active.

What happens if I stop the subscription?

Managed hosting and subscription-based services can be paused when the subscription ends. Specific migration or ownership arrangements can be discussed separately.
