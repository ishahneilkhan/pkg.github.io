05 — Questions

Good to know

What is included in every package?

Every plan includes hosting and SSL, a fully responsive design, custom domain connection and support. The packages above list exactly what is added at each level.

How do I pay?

You can pay by bKash, Nagad or bank transfer. Message us on WhatsApp and we will confirm the details with you.

What is the difference between Starter, Business and Premium?

Starter is a complete ready-made website. Business adds an admin panel, advanced Firebase, inquiry and order management, and backups. Premium is a full Shopify-style system with inventory, orders, coupons and checkout.

Can I see a website before I choose it?

Yes. Every showcase website opens in a live preview, so you can scroll through the real thing before you decide.

How do I ask a question or place an order?
